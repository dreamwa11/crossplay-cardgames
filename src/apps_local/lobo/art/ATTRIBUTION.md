# Lobo artwork

- **Suits and marks** (`moon`, `paw`, `fang`, `pine`, `bone`, `wolf`, `howl`,
  `you`, `cards`, `menu`, `forearm`, `discard`): game-icons.net, by Lorc,
  Delapouite and contributors, CC BY 3.0. The same icons as the phone version.
- **The Wolf** (`wolf.webp`): the phone version's portrait, generated with
  FLUX.1 Krea on Hugging Face. Converted for the e-ink panel by
  `tools_local/lobo/gen_lobo_art.sh`: sharpened, error-diffused to black and
  white and outlined; his hat is cut out of the same picture.
- **The room behind him**: the beamed cellar drawn by
  `tools_local/scoundrel/draw_rooms.py`, at half strength.
