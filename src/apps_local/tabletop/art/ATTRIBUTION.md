# Tabletop artwork

- **Major arcana** (`rws/`): Pamela Colman Smith's drawings for the
  Rider-Waite-Smith deck (Rider, 1909), public domain (US: published 1909;
  UK/EU: Smith died 1951). From Steve P.'s restored scans of a 1909 Pam-A
  deck, via github.com/mixvlad/TarotCards; reduced to line art by the
  generator.
- **Tarot suits** (`wands`, `cups`, `swords`, `pentacles`), **playing suits**
  and the **joker**: game-icons.net, by Lorc, Skoll and Delapouite,
  CC BY 3.0.
- **Crystal ball** (`crystal_ball`, the oracle's menu tile): game-icons.net,
  by Lorc, CC BY 3.0.
- **App icon** (`shelf`, `shelf_small`): a card and dice drawn for this app,
  with the spade from game-icons.net (Skoll, CC BY 3.0).
- **Card backs** (`back0` .. `back2`): The Pale Motel's three backs
  (diamonds, chevrons, rays), its red drawn as paper.
- **Dice** (`d4` .. `d100`): plain outlines drawn for this app.

`tools_local/tabletop/gen_tabletop_art.py` turns them into TabletopArt.h.
