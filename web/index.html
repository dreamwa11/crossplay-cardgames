CrossPlay card games

The black-and-white games and tools from the CrossPlay firmware for the M5Stack PaperMono, playable on a phone. Open one and use Add to Home Screen; open it once from the icon while online and it plays offline from then on.

ScoundrelThe solo dungeon crawl from a deck of cards.
LoboClear the Wolf's hand. Every move has a price.
SeveranceRefine numbers in pairs until every innie finds their outie.
TabletopPlaying cards, tarot and dice, for any table game.
 Scoundrel by Zach Gage and Kurt Bieg (2011). Lobo by James Ernest, Crab Fragment Labs (2022). Severance: The Card Game by u/DREAMWALL, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Unofficial fan versions. Icons from game-icons.net, CC BY 3.0. Tarot drawings by Pamela Colman Smith (1909), public domain.
